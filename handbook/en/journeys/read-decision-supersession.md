# Read decision supersession

<!-- handbook:provenance -->
> Snapshot of devkit `v0.1.6` at source commit `a6e769c5721d`, generated 2026-11-13. The Living Spec under `docs/` prevails on any conflict; this page is not updated between refreshes.

Open an ADR in Read or rendered Files to inspect Supersedes and Superseded by below its header. Whole replacement follows the earlier decision's status and reciprocal metadata; other relations show partial replacement and the written clause scope.

Follow a resolved decision link in the same viewer. Missing or ambiguous identities remain visible without a link; absent scope is shown explicitly. PADRs show outgoing Proposed supersession and their Planning source, without becoming applied incoming ADR relations.

While indexing or partial reads are pending, the document body stays readable. Incomplete results list failed paths and offer Reload relations. An incomplete empty list proves no absence of incoming relations. Switching source or branch discards old-context results. Reading changes no repository files.
