The proposal review report contract in `CAP-DLV-proposal-incubation.md` now reflects the streamlined template:
three mandatory bilingual sections for outcome, decisions and proposed approval effects (identifying recommended
development effort), with compact PADR requirements (problem and choice mandatory; rejected alternatives and
consequences optional). Boundary impacts, planning effort/support, open decisions/waivers, and unverified areas
remain supported as optional sections.
