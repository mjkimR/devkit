# ADR body metadata removal reconciliation

## Contracts

`ADR-frontmatter-only-decision-status` records the decision; its two traced clauses now cite their restating
documents instead of planned wording.

- `CAP-DLV-proposal-planning` §4.4: the PADR scaffold body is the H1 followed by the six sections, and the PADR body
  MUST carry no Status, Date, Deciders or Involved Components line. Implementation: `scaffold.py` `_padr`; test:
  `test_proposal_scaffold.py` asserts no `- **Status**`, `- **Date**` or `---` below the H1 and the H1 directly
  followed by `## 1. Context & Problem Statement`.
- `CAP-DLV-proposal-lifecycle` §4.1 step 4: promotion changes the status only in the frontmatter, writes no body status
  line and deletes the legacy scaffold block (first visible `- **Status**: Draft|Proposed` in the first list after
  the H1, a directly following `- **Date**:` line, and a following `---` when the list is left empty); fenced,
  indented and commented lines stay. Implementation: `handoff.py` `_accept_status` and `_drop_legacy_block`; tests:
  the five promotion tests in `test_proposal_handoff.py` (block deleted with `Proposed`, list and `---` kept when
  another item remains, fenced and commented lines unchanged, trailing-comment status deleted, a PADR without the
  block keeps its body byte for byte apart from the ID rename).
- The docs bundle template and `decision-writing` are agent assets, not Living Spec documents; their contract is the
  ADR and the template test `test_decision_template.py`.
