# Technical Plan: `SPEC-consolidation-terminology` Rename record compaction to consolidation

## 1. Constitution & Invariant Review

- Playbook IDs are a published contract of the `spec` bundle; the rename keeps the old IDs as frontmatter `aliases`,
  which `ADR-playbook-retrieval` defines for exactly this case. No MUST changes.
- Feedback IDs stay resolvable through `aliases` (`CAP-DOCS-document-model` section 3); `devkit rules fix` rewrites
  alias references in current documents.
- The two ledger ADRs change wording only; `ADR-decision-ledger` permits wording edits of an ADR without changing its
  decision. The rename decision itself gets a new ADR (ADR-consolidation-terminology) because it spans document
  kinds and bundles.

## 5. Error Handling & Edge Cases

- `FB-integration-adr-chain-uncompacted` is an observation ID whose word is not the record-merging term; untouched.
- Historical spec bundles cite `SPEC-feedback-dedup-and-compaction` and `SPEC-document-id-aliases-and-compaction`;
  those IDs are history and stay.
- `devkit rules fix` rewrites `FB-compacted-...` references in current documents to the new IDs; references inside
  completed bundles stay and resolve through aliases.
