## Ideas and evaluation limits

The styles emphasize attention, necessary information, direct answers and output that fits the request. The
variants illustrate differing presentation preferences, not a requirement to add presets here. Consider those
ideas through original devkit wording; copy no source wording, examples, distinctive formatting or structure.
Do not infer a user's medical condition or adopt the source's audience assumptions.

The [published benchmark](https://github.com/alexgreensh/attention-span/blob/f5b6e80b48bd9fca4e4b513ef3fc02cb8ec15222/benchmarks/results/2026-08-11-benchmark.md) distinguishes task-test outcomes from
text-shape measurements. Its limits explicitly say readability proxies do not replace a human comprehension
study and test results do not cover open-ended design quality. Its
